# CC0 Pixel Font Sources

The original 19 TrueType fonts in this directory come from SpicyGame's
[SG Font Collection](https://spicygame.itch.io/fonts), version 6 (June 2026).
The collection is released under CC0 1.0 Universal.

Another 20 fonts come from Not Jam's
[Not Jam Font Pack](https://not-jam.itch.io/not-jam-font-pack), version 1.9.0.
The pack is released under CC0 1.0 Universal; its license text is included in
[`NOT-JAM-LICENSE.txt`](NOT-JAM-LICENSE.txt). The included fonts are:

- Not Jam Blackletter 13
- Not Jam Chunky Sans 6
- Not Jam Chunky Sans 8
- Not Jam Faithless 9
- Not Jam Glasgow 13
- Not Jam Laika 11
- Not Jam Old Style 11
- Not Jam Pixel 5
- Not Jam Sci Mono 10
- Not Jam Scrawl 9
- Not Jam Serif 11
- Not Jam Slab Serif 11
- Not Jam Third Dimension 15
- Not Jam Toolkit 15
- Not Jam UI 12
- Not Jam UI Condensed 16
- Undead Pixel Light 8
- Not Jam Mono Prophet 8
- Not Jam Mono Clean 8
- Not Jam Mono Casual 10

The source fonts are rasterized into compact monochrome atlases by
[`tools/build-upgrade-font-atlases.py`](../../../tools/build-upgrade-font-atlases.py).
The generated runtime PNGs and manifest are under
[`PyoroGL/Assets/upgrade-fonts/`](../../../Assets/upgrade-fonts/).
